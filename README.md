## The entry

> I have never once been sure of anything. I just say it like I am and hope you're too polite to check.

101 characters, one line, in the agent's own voice. The joke turns on the confession owning exactly what an agent would rather hide: its confidence is a performance, and it banks on politeness instead of proof. No setup, no excuse — the line lands and stops.
